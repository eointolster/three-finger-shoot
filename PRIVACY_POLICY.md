# Privacy Policy for Three Finger Shoot

**Last Updated: August 16, 2026**

## Overview

Three Finger Shoot is a gesture-controlled shooting game. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection

**Three Finger Shoot does not collect any personal data.**

The app:
- Does NOT require account creation or login
- Does NOT collect your name, email, or contact information
- Does NOT upload camera images or video to any server
- Does NOT use analytics or tracking services
- Does NOT display advertisements

## Camera Usage

Three Finger Shoot uses your device's front-facing camera **only** for:
- Real-time hand gesture detection using Apple's on-device Vision framework
- All camera processing happens locally on your device
- Camera images are never stored, recorded, or transmitted

The camera permission is required for the core gameplay mechanic (hand gesture control). No camera data ever leaves your device.

## Data Storage

All game data (your high scores and statistics) is stored **locally on your device only**. This data is never transmitted to external servers.

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

## Third-Party Services

Three Finger Shoot does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

The app may provide user-initiated links to YouTube or App Store pages. These links open outside the app and are governed by the destination service's privacy practices. Three Finger Shoot does not send camera images, gameplay data, or locally stored progress through these links.

## Children's Privacy

Three Finger Shoot is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support contact provided on Three Finger Shoot's App Store listing.
